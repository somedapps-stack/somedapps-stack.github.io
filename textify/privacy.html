Textify Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026. Textify is a browser extension developed by somedapps.

Summary

Textify has no servers, accounts or analytics of its own. Your data stays in your browser and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives it.

Data stored in your browser

Your snippets, folders, tables, settings, version history and usage counts are saved in the extension's local storage on your device. Uninstalling Textify deletes this storage. Exporting a backup creates a JSON file only where you choose.

Google sign-in and Google Drive (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Textify requests these permissions: your basic profile and email address (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile), and access to files that Textify itself creates in your Drive (drive.file).

  • Your email address and name identify which Google account a library belongs to and are shown in Textify's settings.
  • Textify creates a Textify folder in your Drive and stores JSON backup files of your library there. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Data moves directly between your browser and Google's APIs. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Access tokens stay in the browser's session storage or the browser's sign-in cache. They are not included in exports. Disconnect in Textify revokes them.

Textify's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Textify does not use this data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not let humans read it.

Websites you use

To expand shortcuts, Textify runs on the pages you type in. It does not record or upload your typing or page contents. A template only reads page details, the clipboard, or calls a web address when that template contains a command you wrote for that purpose. Textify does not operate on password fields.

Your choices

  • Use Textify without signing in.
  • Disconnect Google at any time from Settings, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete Textify's backup files from the Textify folder in your Drive.

Children

Textify is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
