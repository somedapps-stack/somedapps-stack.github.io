Textify

A personal text expander for Chrome and Edge.

Textify turns short shortcuts into full text while you type: templates with fields, dropdowns, dates, formulas and nested snippets. It works offline and keeps your library in your browser.

  • Snippets, folders, group shortcuts and a quick search picker
  • Forms, conditions, formulas and local data tables
  • Light and dark themes
  • Optional backup and sync to your own Google Drive

Google sign-in is optional. When you connect Google Drive, Textify stores its backup files in a Textify folder in your own Drive and can only access files it created. Textify has no servers of its own.
